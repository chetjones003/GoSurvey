## What this tracks

Everything GoSurvey's DWG **save** and **open** leave out for **other programs** (AutoCAD, Civil 3D, …). The living inventory is **`docs/dwg-feature-gaps.md`** (updated on `beta` 2026-10-05, commit **`81211cde`**).

GoSurvey saves DWG as **R2000 by default** and can export **R2004–R2018**. When GoSurvey reopens its own DWG it reads the ADR-044 trailer, so GoSurvey users often see a full drawing. **Everyone else only sees the ordinary DWG stream.**

**Status (2026-10-05):** All Group A and Group B checklist items below are **shipped** on `beta`, including **#624** (REQ-371, REQ-372, REQ-385). Optional **#624** follow-ups (**LIGHTLIST**, sun study) are **deferred** and tracked in `docs/dwg-feature-gaps.md`, not as open #601 children. **This tracker is ready to close.**
