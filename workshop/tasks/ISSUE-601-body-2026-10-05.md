## What this tracks

Everything GoSurvey's DWG **save** and **open** leave out for **other programs** (AutoCAD, Civil 3D, …). The living inventory is **`docs/dwg-feature-gaps.md`** (updated on `beta` 2026-10-05).

GoSurvey saves DWG as **R2000 by default** and can export **R2004–R2018**. When GoSurvey reopens its own DWG it reads the ADR-044 trailer, so GoSurvey users often see a full drawing. **Everyone else only sees the ordinary DWG stream.**

**Status (2026-10-05):** Group A and Group B child issues through **#688** (REQ-384, closed) are **shipped** on `beta`. **#624** is **partial:** REQ-371 (visual styles) and REQ-372 (materials diffuse) shipped; **lights/sun** remain a SPEC GAP. This tracker stays **open** until **#624** lights/sun is resolved or moved off the list.
