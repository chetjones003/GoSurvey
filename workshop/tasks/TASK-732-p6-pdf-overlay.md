# TASK-732-p6 - Overlay two revisions of a PDF, lined up by hand (REQ-392, ADR-067 addendum 2, D-2026-10-06-f)

- Status: submitted
- Requirement authority: REQ-392 (accepted, spec PR #741 merged), clauses 1, 2, 4, 5. The roadmap and D-2026-10-06-f put the phases as (6) overlay with **manual** one- and two-point alignment and (7) **automatic** alignment plus change detection, so clause 3 (automatic alignment on opening) and its acceptance test ship with REQ-393 (phase 7), not here. Until then the two pages are laid on each other at their lower-left corners and the bar says so.
- Scope: `src/pdf/PdfAlign` (pure: `Transform`, `FromOnePoint`, `FromTwoPoints`, `Classify` / `TintBgra` / `TintImage`, `ResampleAligned`); `src/ui/PdfCompare` (the comparison view: a one-shot render job on a worker, images uploaded a slice per frame, Tint / Opacity / Blink, one-point and two-point picking, own zoom and pan); viewer toolbar **Compare...** button; `BENCH PDFCOMPARE [pages]` command and `pdfcompare-bench` DevShell test.
- Approach: the job renders the base page and the revision page (revision at the base density times the transform's scale), resamples the revision onto the base's pixel grid, and builds the Tint image; three textures (base, aligned revision, tint) so Opacity and Blink need no new work and the mode switches at once. A new job starts only when what is wanted changes (zoom step, page, alignment); a running one is cancelled. Picking a revision point shows the revision page alone, untransformed, so the point is picked on the real sheet.
- Assumptions (recorded, not asked): annotations are not drawn in the comparison; the comparison shows one base page at a time with its own zoom and pan; ink = luminance under 170 of 255; Tint colours red / blue / dark grey; images are capped at 3000 px on the long side; the page numbers default to the viewer's current page and page 1.
- Technical debt: automatic alignment (REQ-393); the comparison view's pan/zoom code repeats a little of `DrawPages`.
- Verified: 6 unit-test cases / 135 assertions in `tests/PdfAlignTests.cpp` (one-point shift exact, two-point shift + scale + rotation exact, coincident points refused with a reason, inverse round trip, Tint classes on a hand-built 2x2 pair, resample places ink where the transform says and leaves white off the page). `BENCH PDFCOMPARE 500` on the reference machine (DevShell run): first overlay shown 213 ms; per-frame viewer cost over 300 frames of zoom and pan p95 1.26 ms, worst 4.70 ms (target 16 ms). Not verified by a person: how the bar, the picking prompts and the three modes look and feel on real revisions (REQ-392's manual acceptance).
