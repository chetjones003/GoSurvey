- Approach and refinements made while building (recorded in the spec): the outlier rule measures against the **median implied scale**, because measured against the best fit a single wrong check drags the fit and sits right on the limit; Suspect uses the **leave-one-out** residual with the worst removed first, because in a least-squares fit one wrong long dimension drags the fit and makes the good ones look suspect (found by a failing test).
