Angular dimension

---

## Text and angle size

The measurement text is always horizontal. It does not turn with the angle's direction.

An angular dimension measures the angle between its two rays, up to 180 degrees. An angle larger than 180 degrees
(a reflex angle) is not measured; the dimension shows the smaller angle between the two rays. Reflex angles are a
[[Known Limitations]].
