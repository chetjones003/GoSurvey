# CREATEDATASHORTCUT

**Command:** `CREATEDATASHORTCUT`
**Category overview:** [[Point Groups and Surfaces]]

Makes a surface available to other drawings in the project.

`CREATEDATASHORTCUT <surface>`. The source drawing must be saved inside the project, with its
surface changes saved. Other drawings reference it with [[Commands/createsurfacereference]].
