# PADSOLID

**Command:** `PADSOLID` — aliases: `PAD`
**Category overview:** [[Point Groups and Surfaces]]

Builds cut and fill solids between a building pad and a surface.

### Workflow

1. Select ONE closed polyline that bounds the pad (before or after starting).
2. Type `S` to choose the surface, then the **pad elevation**.

Or give both on one line: `PADSOLID <surface>, <pad elevation>`.
