# 3DROTATE

**Command:** `3DROTATE`
**Category overview:** [[Modify Tools]]

Rotates the selected objects with an on-screen 3D gizmo. Works in model space only.

### Workflow

1. **Select objects**, then press **Enter**. Ctrl+click picks a solid's face, edge or vertex.
2. **Base point**: click, snap, or type `X,Y[,Z]`. **Enter** uses the centre of the selection.
3. **Gizmo**: click a ring (X red, Y green, Z blue) to turn about that UCS axis, then click to place or type an angle in degrees.

**Esc** cancels at any step.
