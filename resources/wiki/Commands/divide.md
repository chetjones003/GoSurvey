# DIVIDE


![DIVIDE command overview](wiki-img:commands/divide.png)
**Command:** `DIVIDE` — aliases: `DIV`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Places CAD `POINT` entities that split a picked line, polyline, or circle into N equal segments.

Works in model space and on a paper layout.
