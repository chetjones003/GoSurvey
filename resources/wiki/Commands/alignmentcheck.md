# ALIGNMENTCHECK

**Command:** `ALIGNMENTCHECK`

**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Check an alignment against its design criteria: ALIGNMENTCHECK <alignment>

## Notes

Each finding gives the entity number and the value that fails, for example `Curve radius 250.00' is below the selected minimum design radius of 400.00'.` A finding does not stop the alignment from being saved or used. The same findings appear in ALIGNMENTREPORT under Design criteria.
