# LEADER


![LEADER command overview](wiki-img:commands/leader.png)
**Command:** `LEADER` — aliases: `LE`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Draws a leader from clicked or typed points, then annotation (`Mtext` or `None`). Format chooses a Straight or Spline path and Arrow or None at the tip. Tolerance, Copy, and Block annotation are not supported.

Works in model space and on a paper layout. On a sheet the Mtext box is sized in paper inches; in
model space it is sized in drawing units from the plot scale.
