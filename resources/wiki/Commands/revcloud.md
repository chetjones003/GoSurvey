# REVCLOUD

**Command:** `REVCLOUD`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Draws a revision cloud. Model space only.

### Workflow

1. **First corner**, or an option:
   - `O` (Object): click a closed polyline, rectangle or circle to turn into a cloud.
   - `A` (Arc length): set the arc length.
   - `R` (Reverse): flip the arc direction.
   - `D` (Delete original): in Object mode, choose whether the original shape is deleted or kept.
2. **Next corner**, or `C` (Close), `U` (Undo), `A`, `R`. **Enter** closes the cloud.

A cloud needs at least three corners. Object mode cannot join a path already under way.
