# MEASURE


![MEASURE command overview](wiki-img:commands/measure.png)
**Command:** `MEASURE` — aliases: `ME`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Places CAD `POINT` entities at a typed interval along a picked line, polyline, or circle.

Works in model space and on a paper layout.
