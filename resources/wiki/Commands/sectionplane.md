# SECTIONPLANE

**Command:** `SECTIONPLANE` — aliases: `SPLANE`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Places a section plane.

### Workflow

1. Select a **flat face** of a solid. The plane is placed on it.
2. Or click a **point** to start a section line, then the **through point**. The plane stands on that
   line.

Drag the plane's centre handle to slide it. Curved faces are refused. The drawing must contain at
least one solid. **Esc** cancels.

See also [[Commands/sectionclip]].
