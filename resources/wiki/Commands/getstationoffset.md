# GETSTATIONOFFSET

**Command:** `GETSTATIONOFFSET`
**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Station and offset of a point: GETSTATIONOFFSET <alignment>, <x>, <y>

See [[Alignments]] for the full workflow, the station format, and what Phase 1 does and does not include.

## A survey point

`GETSTATIONOFFSET <alignment>, POINT, <number>` takes a survey point by its point number, and gives its station and offset.
