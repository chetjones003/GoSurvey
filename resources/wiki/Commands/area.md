# AREA


![AREA command overview](wiki-img:commands/area.png)
**Command:** `AREA` — aliases: `AA`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Reports area and perimeter from typed points, or from a picked closed polyline or circle. Add and Subtract keep a running total. Read-only — no undo entry.

Works in model space and on a paper layout.
