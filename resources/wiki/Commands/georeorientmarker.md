# GEOREORIENTMARKER

**Command:** `GEOREORIENTMARKER`
**Category overview:** [[Settings and Options]]

Sets the drawing's geographic marker.

1. **Design point**: click or type `X,Y`.
2. **North**: a point in the north direction (not the design point).

**Esc** cancels.
