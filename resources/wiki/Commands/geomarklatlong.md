# GEOMARKLATLONG

**Command:** `GEOMARKLATLONG`
**Category overview:** [[Settings and Options]]

Places a Position Marker at a latitude and longitude.

1. **Latitude**: decimal degrees (`30.28625`) or D M S with N/S (`30 17 10.5N`).
2. **Longitude**: decimal degrees, west negative (`-97.7394`), or D M S with E/W (`97 44 21.7W`).

**Esc** cancels. See also [[Commands/geomarkpoint]].
