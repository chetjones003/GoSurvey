# ALIGNMENTSPEED

**Command:** `ALIGNMENTSPEED`  
**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Design speed from a station: ALIGNMENTSPEED <alignment>, <station>, <speed>|CLEAR

## Notes

The speed applies from its station onward, until the next speed. A curve or tangent that spans two speeds is checked against the higher one. `CLEAR` removes the speed set at that station. Speeds are unitless numbers in the speed units your radius table uses.
