# 3DMOVE

**Command:** `3DMOVE`
**Category overview:** [[Modify Tools]]

Moves the selected objects with an on-screen 3D gizmo. Works in model space only.

### Workflow

1. **Select objects**, then press **Enter**. Ctrl+click picks a solid's face, edge or vertex.
2. **Base point**: click, snap, or type `X,Y[,Z]`. **Enter** uses the centre of the selection.
3. **Gizmo**: drag an arrow to move along that UCS axis, or a corner square to slide in that plane. Click to place, or type a distance (`dx,dy` for a plane) and press **Enter**.

**Esc** cancels at any step.
