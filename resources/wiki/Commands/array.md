# ARRAY


![ARRAY command overview](wiki-img:commands/array.png)
**Command:** `ARRAY` — aliases: `AR`
**Category overview:** [[Modify Tools]]

Creates rectangular or polar arrays of selected objects. Choose the array type, enter the counts and
spacing or center and fill angle, then choose whether polar copies rotate with the pattern. The
original objects stay in place, and the completed array is one undoable operation.

In a paper layout, `ARRAY` works on selected objects on the active sheet. Its copies remain in that
layout; model geometry and other layouts are unchanged.

See [[Modify Tools]] for related tools and workflows.
