![DIMLINEAR command overview](wiki-img:commands/dimlinear.png)
**Command:** `DIMLINEAR` — aliases: `DLI`
**Category overview:** [[Annotation]]

Linear dimension

See [[Annotation]] for related tools and workflows.

---

## Text

The measurement text reads upright whichever way the two extension points are picked. Picking the right-hand
point first, or the top point first, does not turn the number upside down.

Moving the dimension with its grips keeps the number it had, and the number follows the drawing's unit scale and
precision from the dimension style.
