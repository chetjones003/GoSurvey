# SECTIONCLIP

**Command:** `SECTIONCLIP` — aliases: `SCLIP`, `CLIP`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Clips the view live by the UCS XY plane.

At the prompt, or on the same line (`SECTIONCLIP ON`), enter:

| Input | Effect |
|---|---|
| `ON` | Turn the clip on |
| `OFF` | Turn the clip off |
| `FLIP` | Keep the other side |
| a number | Clip at that offset along the UCS Z axis |

See also [[Commands/sectionplane]].
