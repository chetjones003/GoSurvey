# PROFILEQUERY

**Command:** `PROFILEQUERY`
**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Report a design profile at a station: PROFILEQUERY <profile>, <station>

Reports the elevation, the grade (rise over run, shown as a percent) and the 3D design point (easting, northing, elevation) at the displayed station. A station before the first point or after the last point has no value and says so. A profile whose alignment was deleted is refused with the reason.
