# STATIONFORMAT

**Command:** `STATIONFORMAT`
**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Sets how stations are written in labels and reports.

`STATIONFORMAT [DELIMITER=+|-|_|/|C|K|NONE][, DIGITS=1..6][, DECIMALS=0..6][, PREFIX=<text>][, SUFFIX=<text>]`

`STATIONFORMAT DEFAULT` restores the default. The command answers with a sample station.
