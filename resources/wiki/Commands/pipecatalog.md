# PIPECATALOG

**Command:** `PIPECATALOG` — aliases: `PCAT`
**Category overview:** [[Drawing Tools]]

Looks up a fitting in the fitting library.

`PIPECATALOG <part type> <nominal size> [pressure class]`, e.g. `PIPECATALOG tee 4in CS150`.

Part types: `elbow-90`, `elbow-45`, `tee`, `cross`, `reducer`, `flange`, `valve`, `coupling`, `cap`,
`nozzle`, `other`. An ambiguous match lists the candidates.
