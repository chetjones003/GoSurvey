# MVIEW


![MVIEW command overview](wiki-img:commands/mview.png)
**Command:** `MVIEW` — aliases: `RECTVIEWPORT`, `RECTVP`
**Category overview:** [[Paper Space and Layouts]]

Create paper-space viewports with `MVIEW`. Click two opposite corners for a rectangle, or enter an option:

- `Fit` fills the full sheet rectangle edge to edge.
- `2` creates two equal side-by-side viewports.
- `3` creates one full-height viewport on the left and two stacked viewports on the right.
- `4` creates an equal 2×2 grid.
- `Object` uses a selected closed paper-space polyline as the viewport boundary. The source polyline stays in place.

Press Esc to cancel an active MVIEW prompt. These options add viewports to the current paper layout.

See [[Paper Space and Layouts]] for related tools and workflows.
