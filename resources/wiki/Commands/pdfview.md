# PDFVIEW

**Command:** `PDFVIEW` - aliases: `PV`
**Category overview:** [[Import and Export]]

Opens a PDF in GoSurvey's own viewer window. Double-clicking a PDF in the Project tab does the same.
The first page shows almost at once, even for a several-hundred-page file, and pages are drawn ahead
of where you scroll so scrolling stays smooth.

- Scroll to move, **Ctrl + wheel** to zoom, drag to pan.
- Page box, `<` / `>`, Page Up / Page Down, Home / End to change page.
- **Fit width**, **Fit page**, a typed zoom %, **Continuous** (one long scroll) or single page.
- A **Thumbnails** strip on the left.

`BENCH PDFVIEW [pages]` times the viewer on a generated PDF (default 500 pages).
