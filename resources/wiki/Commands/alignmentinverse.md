# ALIGNMENTINVERSE

**Command:** `ALIGNMENTINVERSE`
**Category overview:** [[Alignments]]

Distance along an alignment: ALIGNMENTINVERSE <alignment>, STA, <station1>, <station2>[, CHORD] | <alignment>, PT, <x1>, <y1>, <x2>, <y2>[, CHORD]

Gives the distance along the alignment between two stations, or between two points projected onto it. For a curve that is the arc length. CHORD also gives the straight-line distance between the two points.
