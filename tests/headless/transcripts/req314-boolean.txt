# REQ-314 / ADR-046 increment 4 B1 (GitHub #147, Phase 4 of #120) — UNION / SUBTRACT / INTERSECT.
#
# BrepTests proves the kernel. This transcript tests the commands: two selected solids are combined
# and replaced by the result in one undo step, and a pair the kernel refuses is reported with the
# document unchanged.

NEW

# A: x[-5,5] y[-5,5] z[0,10] (volume 1000).  B: x[-1,9] y[-5,5] z[4,14] (volume 1000).
# Overlap 6 x 10 x 6 = 360.  Union 1640, subtract (A-B) 640, intersect 360.
CMD BOX 0,0 10 10 10
CMD BOX 4,0,4 10 10 10
EXPECT SOLIDS 2

# --- UNION ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BOX -12 -8 12 8
EXPECT SELECTED 2
CMD UNION
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
EXPECT LOG "combined into 1 solid"
CMD SOLIDLIST
EXPECT LOG "volume 1640"

# --- Undo restores the two operands -------------------------------------------------------
CMD UNDO
EXPECT SOLIDS 2

# --- SUBTRACT: A - B ---------------------------------------------------------------------
BOX -12 -8 12 8
EXPECT SELECTED 2
CMD SUBTRACT
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
CMD SOLIDLIST
EXPECT LOG "volume 640"

CMD UNDO
EXPECT SOLIDS 2

# --- INTERSECT ------------------------------------------------------------------------
BOX -12 -8 12 8
EXPECT SELECTED 2
CMD INTERSECT
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
CMD SOLIDLIST
EXPECT LOG "volume 360"

CMD UNDO
EXPECT SOLIDS 2

# --- Three solids: UNION folds them into one --------------------------------------------
CMD BOX 8,0,0 6 10 10
EXPECT SOLIDS 3
BOX -12 -8 15 8
EXPECT SELECTED 3
CMD UNION
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
EXPECT LOG "3 solids combined into 1 solid"
CMD UNDO
EXPECT SOLIDS 3

# --- Fewer than two solids selected: reported, nothing changes --------------------------
NEW
CMD BOX 0,0 4 4 4
BOX -3 -3 3 3
EXPECT SELECTED 1
CMD INTERSECT
EXPECT LOG "at least two solids"
EXPECT SOLIDS 1

# --- A curved operand is refused -------------------------------------------------------
NEW
CMD BOX 0,0 6 6 6
CMD CYLINDER 0,0 3 10
BOX -8 -8 8 8
EXPECT SELECTED 2
CMD UNION
EXPECT LOG "flat faces only"
EXPECT SOLIDS 2

# --- .gs round-trip of a boolean result ---------------------------------------------
NEW
CMD BOX 0,0 10 10 10
CMD BOX 4,0,4 10 10 10
BOX -12 -8 12 8
CMD SUBTRACT
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
SAVEAS %OUT%/req314-boolean.gs
OPEN %OUT%/req314-boolean.gs
EXPECT SOLIDS 1
CMD SOLIDLIST
EXPECT LOG "volume 640"

CHECK ALL
